🐾Lenny’s little adventure
HELLO, LITTLE EXPLORER!

Grown-ups

For Prep kids aged 5–6. Inspired by Alpha-style life-skill workshops: leadership and teamwork, storytelling and public speaking, entrepreneurship and money, relationships, and grit. Each stop has a 10–15 minute mission. Children plan, make, test or discuss a choice, then explain what they learned. Instructions appear one at a time, with a practical show-or-tell goal. A completed show-or-tell mission earns a saved pixel meat treat. Feeding a treat to Lenny triggers the surprise celebration. Children or a grown-up confirm completion. Children can answer each mission by voice or typing, ask for help, and say “next” or “home”. Lenny acknowledges the answer and asks a mission question. Children or their grown-up decide whether the goal has been met. After five missions, children create, test and present a project for a real person. A grown-up can help with materials, join in or celebrate their effort.

This player’s badges, treats and secret hearts will be cleared.

Ask a grown-up before using the microphone. After you tap it, Lenny listens again after each reply. Home, muting sound or saying “stop” ends the chat. This site uses built-in conversations, pre-recorded Australian-style female voice clips for special moments, and browser speech for other dialogue or instructions. Your browser may send audio to its speech service. No fal or other API key is needed on the live site. Nicknames, adventure progress and earned treats stay in this browser. Found hearts are saved with each nickname. Six hidden hearts give Lenny a hug animation, with a bigger message after all six are discovered. The site does not save questions or recordings. Task instructions are read aloud when selected. Tap the task title or Read it again to hear the current step once more. The prerecorded voice uses hosted clips when the local copies have not been installed. Other spoken instructions rely on an Australian English browser voice where available. Voice choice and microphone support vary by device. Keep personal details out of questions.
