LENNY'S LITTLE ADVENTURE — GITHUB PAGES VERSION

CONTENTS
index.html is the complete website. Pictures, pixel art, styles, game logic and activities are embedded.

PUBLISH
1. Upload index.html to the root of your GitHub repository.
2. Open Settings > Pages.
3. Publish from your chosen branch and the root folder.
4. Open the HTTPS website address in Safari on iPad or iPhone.

PLAY
Every visit starts with a name or nickname. The same nickname returns to that player's progress.
Different nicknames have separate progress and saved treats in the same browser.
Five real-world missions lead to a child-chosen project.
Children can speak answers, ask for help, or say next and home.
Completing a mission earns a pixel meat treat. Tap Feed Lenny to send it to his mouth.
The surprise sunglasses, unicorn, rainbow, rocket, crown or star reward happens after feeding.
Treats can be saved for later. Badges, treats and progress survive a reload.

CONNECTIONS AND VOICE STATUS
The current downloadable version uses the device's slow browser speech voice.
The funnier generated character voice is NOT included yet.
Fal is now connected, but its account is out of generation credits. Add credits in the fal dashboard before the character-voice samples can be generated and added.
No fal key or other credentials are included in this static website.
Speech recognition uses browser support and asks for microphone permission.
The browser may process microphone audio through its own online speech service.

TOUCH AND DEVICE CHECKS
The layout was checked at phone and tablet widths with large touch controls.
Actual iPad/iPhone Safari microphone and audio playback still need a real-device check.
Tap a button to start audio. Open Safari directly if an in-app browser cannot play or listen.
Typing and showing someone your work are available when voice input is unavailable.
Reduced-motion preferences are respected.

DATA AND PLAYERS
Nicknames, progress and treats are stored locally in the browser.
Spoken answers and recordings are not saved by the website.
These are local play profiles, not password-protected accounts.
Clearing browser data removes the saved profiles.
Grown-ups can switch players or reset the current adventure.

LEARNING AND COMPLETION
The missions draw on leadership/teamwork, storytelling/public speaking, entrepreneurship/money,
relationships and grit. Children plan, create, test, improve and explain their choices.
Completion is confirmed by the child or grown-up. Spoken answers receive built-in follow-up prompts,
not automated assessment.

SECRET HEART HUNT
Six small pixel hearts are hidden around the page. Tap or press one to get a lion hug.
Each discovery is saved per nickname. Finding all six gives a bigger hug message.
Previously found hearts can be pressed for another hug without increasing the count.
Resetting the current adventure clears that player’s hearts as well as badges and treats.
